05 · Review and scientific reflection Learning summary
Experiment debrief

What did the simulation demonstrate?

Filtration is a physical separation technique. Compare the material held by the filter with the liquid that passed through it.

◌
Residue

Suspended insoluble leaf particles retained by the filter paper.

◉
Filtrate

Liquid that passes through the filter, potentially including dissolved substances.

!
Evidence matters

Filtration alone does not prove insecticidal effectiveness or safety.

Scientific limitation: filtration removes suspended insoluble particles, but dissolved compounds can remain in the filtrate. Any insect-control claim requires controlled efficacy tests, appropriate controls, and a separate safety assessment.
